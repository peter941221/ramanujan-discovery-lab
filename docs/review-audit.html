Conclusion

After benchmark-relative equivalence dedupe, none of the six public `review` candidates is strong enough to be called a new formula. Two remain worth watching as weak leads; the rest currently look family-adjacent.

Why so conservative?

Generalized Ramanujan-style continued fractions are already a broad literature. A quick exact-string non-hit is not enough to declare novelty.
