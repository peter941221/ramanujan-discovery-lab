Conclusion

None of the six `review` candidates is strong enough to be called a new formula publicly. Two remain worth watching as weak leads.

watch family-adjacent

cb60fd71d1d7

closest: rogers_ramanujan_q3_normalized (7 digits)

Structured denominator perturbation of the `q^3` Rogers-Ramanujan family. Interesting, but still too close to a known family for a novelty claim.

watch family-adjacent

1125ffe48b3b

closest: shifted_rr_fixture (6 digits)

A mixed numerator-plus-denominator perturbation of a Rogers-Ramanujan style family. Worth a second pass, but still not a publishable discovery claim.

Why so conservative?

Generalized Ramanujan-style continued fractions are already a broad literature. A quick exact-string non-hit is not enough to declare novelty.
